FAQ

Questions? Start here

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 11:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes! Dogs and cats stay free, with no extra charge.

I don't have a tent. Can I still stay?

Yes. You can rent a tent for 1,000–1,200 THB, including 2 sets of bedding and a fan — or book a cabin (from 1,500 THB per night).

Is there electricity at the campground?

Yes, there are power hook-up points for the camping lawn.

What's included for free?

Fishing from the lakeshore, a free 1-hour canoe per person, life jackets, and free Wi‑Fi.

Can I canoe for longer than an hour?

Yes. The first hour is free, then it's 100 THB per person for each extra hour.

How do I book and pay?

Choose your dates and an available cabin on the booking page, then pay a 50% deposit or the full amount by PromptPay QR within 6 hours and send us the slip on LINE. You can also call +66 81 930 4969.

I don't have a Thai bank account. How can I pay?

No problem — you can pay in cash. Message us on LINE or Facebook with your dates instead of booking online, and our admin will reserve your cabin for you.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Once paid, contact our admin to change your dates. If you cancel or don't show up: with a 50% deposit, the deposit is not refunded; with full payment, you get 50% of the booking amount back.

Are there quiet hours?

Yes — please keep noise down after 22:00 so everyone can rest.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's a wide lawn with parking. Rooftop-tent campers are welcome too.

Are you open every day?

Yes, we're open every day. Please book or call ahead, especially over long holidays.
