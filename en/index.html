Welcome

Stay by the water, under big shady trees

The Lagoon has a wide lakeside lawn for tents (with power hook-up points) and 6 cabins — from small cabins for two to family houses with a kitchen.

Paddle a canoe in the morning, watch the sunset by the lake in the evening. Perfect for camping crews, families, and anyone who never travels without their pet.
